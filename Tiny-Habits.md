# Tiny Habits

## Question 1: Most Interesting Idea from Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg)

The most interesting idea is that small habits work better than big goals. If you make a habit tiny and celebrate after doing it, your brain starts to like it and it becomes automatic.

---

## Question 2: B = MAP Formula

* **B = MAP** means Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and Prompt come together at the same time.
* **M (Motivation):** Your desire to do the behavior.
* **A (Ability):** How easy or hard the behavior is for you.
* **P (Prompt):** The cue or trigger that reminds you to do it.

To make new habits easier, increase **Ability** by making the habit very small and simple, and use a clear **Prompt** like an existing daily action.

---

## Question 3: Why Celebrate ("Shine") After a Habit?

Celebrating creates a positive emotion that tells your brain, **"This is good, do it again."**

This feeling of **"Shine"** helps lock the habit into your mind so it becomes automatic over time.

---

## Question 4: Most Interesting Idea from "1% Better Every Day"

The most interesting idea is that improving just **1% every day** can make you about **37 times better in a year** because small gains compound over time.

---

## Question 5: Book's Perspective on Identity (Atomic Habits)

The book says real change comes from changing your **identity**, not only your goals.

Your habits are votes for the type of person you want to become.

---

## Question 6: How to Make a Habit Easier (Atomic Habits)

* Make the habit very small using the **two-minute rule**.
* Reduce friction and prepare your environment in advance.
* Use clear cues and stack the new habit onto an existing one.
* Automate or simplify the steps so it feels easy to start.

---

## Question 7: How to Make a Habit Harder (Atomic Habits)

* Add friction, such as extra steps before doing the habit.
* Remove or hide the cues that trigger the habit.
* Use commitment devices, like blocking apps or logging out.
* Make the reward less satisfying or add a cost to the behavior.

---

## Question 8: One Habit I Want to Do More

I want to **practise coding every day**.

* **Cue:** After I finish my morning tea, I will open my IDE.
* **Make it attractive:** I will start with a small, enjoyable problem.
* **Make it easy:** I will code for only 10 minutes at first.
* **Make it satisfying:** I will tick a habit tracker and celebrate after finishing.

---

## Question 9: One Habit I Want to Do Less

I want to **reduce scrolling on my phone late at night**.

* **Make the cue invisible:** I will keep my phone outside the bedroom.
* **Make it unattractive:** I will remind myself that it makes me tired the next day.
* **Make it hard:** I will log out of social media apps and use a screen-time limit.
* **Make the response unsatisfying:** I will track my screen time and feel bad if I exceed my limit.
