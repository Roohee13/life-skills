# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep work means working on important, mentally demanding tasks without any distractions. It pushes your brain to its limit and helps you learn faster and produce better results.

---

## Question 2: How to Do Deep Work Properly (Key Points)

- Work in a distraction-free environment.
- Schedule fixed time blocks for deep work.
- Embrace boredom instead of reaching for your phone.
- Quit or limit social media use.
- Reduce shallow work like unnecessary emails and meetings.
- Train your focus like a muscle with regular practice.
- Use rituals to start deep work sessions easily.

---

## Question 3: How I Can Implement These Principles

- I will block 1–2 hours daily for deep work on coding or learning.
- I will keep my phone on silent and away from my desk.
- I will turn off non-essential notifications on my laptop.
- I will use a timer and work in focused sprints.
- I will schedule social media use only after my deep work session.
- I will track my deep work hours and improve gradually.
- I will take short breaks without using my phone.

---

## Question 4: Dangers of Social Media (Brief)

- It reduces focus and increases distraction.
- It creates addiction through endless scrolling and notifications.
- It causes comparison and lowers self-esteem.
- It spreads misinformation and false content quickly.
- It harms privacy by collecting personal data.
- It can waste large amounts of time that could be used for deep work.
