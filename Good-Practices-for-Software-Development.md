# Good Practices for Software Development

## Question 1: Which Point(s) Were New to You?

- Writing and sharing clear requirement notes even if the team does not use tools like Jira or Trello.
- Over-communicating proactively about delays, internet issues, or unexpected problems.
- Asking questions in a structured way with screenshots, code snippets, and steps already tried.
- Joining meetings 5–10 minutes early to connect with teammates informally.
- Sending all questions in one clear message instead of many small messages.
- Using deep work principles like blocking social media and tracking time during work hours.

## Question 2: Areas to Improve and My Ideas to Make Progress

### *Area 1: Clearer Communication*

- I will write short, clear messages with all details in one place.
- I will use screenshots or short videos when explaining a problem.
- I will over-communicate early if I see any risk to the deadline.

### *Area 2: Asking Better Questions*

- Before asking, I will list what I have already tried.
- I will share error messages, code snippets, and steps to reproduce the issue.
- I will use tools like GitHub Gist or CodeSandbox to share my setup.

### *Area 3: Focus and Deep Work*

- I will keep my phone on silent and remove non-work notifications.
- I will block social media during work hours using an app.
- I will track my work time and review it daily to improve focus.

### *Area 4: Team Connection*

- I will join meetings a few minutes early to talk with teammates.
- I will learn my teammates’ working hours and schedule calls accordingly.
- I will send thoughtful, well-structured messages instead of many small ones.
