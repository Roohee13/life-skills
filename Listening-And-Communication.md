# Listening and Communication

## Question 1: Active Listening

1. Give the speaker your full attention.
2. Avoid using your phone or getting distracted.
3. Do not interrupt.
4. Look at the speaker while they are talking.
5. Ask questions if something is unclear.
6. Take notes during important discussions.
7. Show that you are listening by nodding or saying, “I understand.”
8. Repeat the main points to confirm your understanding.

## Question 2: Reflective Listening

* Listen carefully to the speaker’s words and feelings.
* Understand the main message.
* Repeat the message in your own words.
* Ask questions to clear up confusion.
* Confirm whether your understanding is correct.
* Correct yourself if you misunderstood something.

## Question 3: Obstacles in My Listening

* I sometimes get distracted by my phone.
* I think about my reply before the person finishes.
* I sometimes interrupt.
* I may lose focus during long discussions.
* I sometimes hesitate to ask questions.
* My personal opinions can affect my understanding.

## Question 4: How I Can Improve My Listening

* Keep my phone silent during conversations.
* Give the speaker my full attention.
* Avoid interrupting.
* Take notes in meetings.
* Ask questions when I do not understand.
* Repeat important points in my own words.
* Share and check meeting notes with the team.

## Question 5: When I Use Passive Communication

I use passive communication when I feel nervous, want to avoid conflict, or do not want to hurt someone’s feelings. Sometimes I stay quiet or agree with others even when I have a different opinion.

## Question 6: When I Use Aggressive Communication

I may use aggressive communication when I am angry, stressed, or under pressure. I may speak rudely, blame someone, or try to force my opinion on others.

## Question 7: When I Use Passive-Aggressive Communication

I may use passive-aggressive communication when I am upset but do not express my feelings directly. I may use sarcasm, give silent treatment, complain to others, or delay the work intentionally.

## Question 8: How I Can Communicate Assertively

* Speak clearly and politely.
* Use **“I” statements**, such as “I feel stressed.”
* Explain my needs directly.
* Listen to the other person’s opinion.
* Keep my voice calm.
* Say **“no”** respectfully when necessary.
* Discuss problems early instead of hiding my feelings.
* Focus on finding a solution instead of blaming someone.

### Example

> “I have many tasks today. Can we decide which one I should complete first?”
