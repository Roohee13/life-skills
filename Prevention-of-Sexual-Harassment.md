# **Prevention of Sexual Harassment**

## **Question 1: What Kinds of Behaviour Cause Sexual Harassment?**

**Sexual harassment** includes any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, humiliated, or unsafe.

**Some examples are:**

* **Unwelcome physical contact** like touching, hugging, massaging, or brushing against someone.
* **Sexual comments, jokes, or remarks** about a person’s body, appearance, or private life.
* **Suggestive looks, staring, leering, or sexual gestures.**
* **Demands or requests for sexual favours**, including promises in return for favours.
* **Showing or sharing sexually explicit images, posts, or messages.**
* **Spreading sexual rumours** or asking intrusive questions about someone’s sex life.
* **Unwanted flirting, repeated advances, or asking someone out** despite clear refusal.
* **Stalking, cyberflashing, or any form of sexual assault.**

Harassment can be **verbal, non-verbal, physical, or online**. It creates a **hostile or unsafe work environment**.

## **Question 2: What Would You Do If You Face or Witness Such Behaviour?**

### **If I Face Sexual Harassment:**

* If I feel safe, I will **clearly tell the person that their behaviour is unwanted and must stop.**
* I will **write down details of each incident**, including the date, time, place, what happened, and witnesses.
* I will report the incident to my **manager, HR, or the Internal Complaints Committee** as per company policy.
* I will seek support from a **trusted colleague, union representative, or external helpline** if needed.

### **If I Witness Sexual Harassment:**

* If it is safe, I will **speak up and tell the harasser that the behaviour is inappropriate.**
* I will **support the person being harassed** and let them know I am on their side.
* I will **offer to be a witness** if they decide to file a complaint.
* I will **report the incident to management or HR**, especially if it is repeated or severe.
* I will **not laugh at or encourage inappropriate jokes or comments.**

In all cases, I will follow my company’s **PoSH policy** and **cooperate with any investigation**.
