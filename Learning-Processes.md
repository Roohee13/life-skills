# Learning Processes

## Question 1
### What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

**Answer:**
The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a topic in very simple words, as if you are teaching it to a child.

---

## Question 2
### In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

**Answer:**
The most interesting idea is that learning works better when you use two brain modes: **focus** and **diffuse**. In focus mode, you solve problems directly, and in diffuse mode, your brain relaxes and connects ideas in the background.

---

## Question 3
### What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Answer:**
- **Active (Focused) Mode:** You concentrate hard on one problem and think through it step by step.
- **Diffuse Mode:** Your mind is relaxed, allowing it to quietly make new connections and generate ideas.

---

## Question 4
### According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

**Answer:**
1. Deconstruct the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove barriers and distractions.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours.

---

## Question 5
### What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

**Answer:**
- Study with full attention and avoid distractions.
- Use the Feynman Technique and explain concepts in your own words.
- Test yourself often instead of only reading.
- Break topics into small parts and practice them.
- Set deadlines, but use them to manage time rather than stress yourself.
- Take short, focused work sessions and use breaks effectively.
